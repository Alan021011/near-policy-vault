# Threat model (v1.7)

Approved by the author on 2026-11-04 before any contract code was written; updated on 2026-11-05 after the M3 review. "Planned" means a mitigation exists on paper only; "Partial" says which part is tested. Updated again on 2026-11-05 after M5 (spending policy), M6 (proposal queue), the M6 review M7 (signing from the contract, end to end on Sepolia), the M7 review and the general review after M7. **The contract signs since M7**, so the open threats (1 before a sale, 16) are live risks on testnet. The contract cannot sign anything yet (M6-M7), so no funds are at risk today; the two-phase sale (M8) is not built, so threats 1, 5 and 16 are still unmitigated in code.

## Assets and actors

- **Asset:** funds held at the EVM address derived for each NFT.
- **Owner:** the current holder of the NFT. **Seller / buyer:** the old and new owner around a sale.
- **Guardian:** one NEAR account per vault that can veto a queued transaction.
- **MPC network:** the NEAR Chain Signatures signers (8 nodes according to the NEAR documentation).

## Core rule: validate what you sign

The contract stores the transaction fields (chain id, nonce, fees, gas limit, destination, value, data), checks the policy on those fields and computes the EIP-1559 signing hash itself. A caller never supplies a hash. Only plain ETH transfers and ERC-20 `transfer` / `approve` calls are ever built. Message signing (permits, EIP-712) and EIP-7702 authorizations are never produced.

## Threats

| # | Threat | Planned mitigation | State |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Pre-signed transaction.** The seller obtains a valid signature, sells the NFT and broadcasts the transaction afterwards | Two-phase sale: `start_sale` freezes new signatures; the buyer audits the external-chain nonce against the contract's `next_nonce`; if a signed transaction is outstanding, a cancel transaction with the same nonce is signed; only then does `nft_transfer` succeed | Partial: `cancel_nonce` exists and was proven on Sepolia (a signed, unbroadcast payment became unminable: "nonce too low"); the two-phase sale that forces it before a transfer is M8 |
| 2 | The seller adds themself as an allowed destination and drains the cap before selling | Every change of owner deletes the policy, its pending change and its spending windows; the daily cap bounds the damage | Partial: policy and queue reset tested (unit + testnet) |
| 3 | Misreading of calldata lets a dangerous approval through | Hash is built from validated fields; golden tests compare the Rust hash with viem for the same fields | Partial: the hash matches viem on 19 vectors, the strict classifier and the policy are unit-tested, and `check_transaction` refuses bad approvals on testnet; no ERC-20 call has been signed and mined live yet |
| 4 | A bug makes the contract sign something it should not | A single signing path; an attack suite that tries each bypass; manual review | Partial: one signing path (`execute_proposal`), which only signs the hash it computed from a proposal that passed the policy; the callback also checks the MPC signature against the vault key with `ecrecover`. Attack suite still to come |
| 5 | The `sign` callback sees a different owner than the one who requested it | Each proposal records the owner epoch; transfers are blocked while a signature is in flight | Mitigated: while a signature is in flight the NFT cannot be transferred and no second signature can start (unit tests); proposals also carry the owner epoch |
| 6 | Race on the daily cap | Charge the caps when a proposal is committed for signing, in one synchronous step with the nonce; give it back only if the answer provably cannot spend (wrong key). Windows are fixed 24 h: across a window boundary up to 2x the cap can be signed (declared) | Partial: caps are charged when a proposal is committed for signing, in one synchronous step with the nonce (review P1, reproduced and fixed with a unit test); live on Sepolia since M7 |
| 7 | Permissions the seller left on the external chain | Only approvals to allowed spenders up to the token's `max_approval` can be signed; amounts above 128 bits are refused, so 2^256-1 and 2^255 both fail | Partial: `check_transaction` refuses them on testnet and only policy-checked transactions are signed; no live ERC-20 approve signed yet |
| 8 | Trust in the MPC network | Not solved; declared. The contract does check that every signature it stores recovers to the vault key, so a wrong or tampered answer is rejected and undone (unit tests with the real A1 signature) | Residual |
| 9 | Whoever controls the contract account can change the contract | Today the deployer keeps a full-access key (used for `migrate`). Before submission: delete it and show "0 full access keys"; declared in the README | Pending (documented) |
| 10 | Stuck nonce or missing gas on the external chain | The nonce is assigned at signing time (cancelled proposals leave no gaps); fees are fixed when proposing, so a proposal with stale fees must be cancelled and proposed again; the cancel transaction in threat 1 helps. Transactions that can never be mined (gas limit below 21000, zero fee) are refused (review P2). A stuck nonce can be re-signed (`replace_nonce`) or cancelled (`cancel_nonce`) | Partial |
| 11 | EIP-7702 authorizations or message signatures obtained from the contract | The contract only ever builds and hashes EIP-1559 (type 2) transactions with one of three calldata shapes; there is no path that signs a message, a permit or an authorization | Mitigated by construction |
| 12 | Replay across chains | `chain_id` is inside the EIP-1559 hash, so a signature for one chain is invalid on another. The derivation path (`vault-<id>`) is the same for every EVM chain, so each vault has the same address everywhere; the policy must fix which chain ids a vault may sign for | Mitigated: `chain_id` is in the hash (tested against viem); the policy names one chain; the first signature fixes the vault's chain for good, also across sales (general review G2, checked live) |
| 13 | During `nft_transfer_call` the receiver owns the NFT for a moment and may hand it back | The sale freeze and the "signature in flight" lock must treat a pending `transfer_call` as a transfer; ownership changes are recorded (`vault_transfer_info`) | Partial: behaviour checked on testnet; transfers (also `transfer_call`) are blocked while a signature is in flight; the sale freeze is M8 |
| 14 | An approved account (e.g. a marketplace) moves the NFT | Every transfer path, by the owner or by an approved account, goes through the same wrapper where the locks will live; approvals are cleared on transfer and their deposit refunded | Partial: wrapper, clearing and the in-flight lock tested; the sale freeze is M8 |
| 15 | A token id is reused, so two owners share one EVM account | Ids are sequential and never reissued; there is no burn | Tested (unit test) |
| 16 | **Stolen owner key.** A thief with the owner's NEAR key transfers the NFT to an account of theirs; the transfer clears the policy and the first policy of a locked vault applies at once, so the delay and the guardian are skipped | M8: a transfer must wait the current delay and be vetoable by the guardian (sale phase), so the guardian also protects against theft of the NFT | **Open and live since M7** (found while building M5). Highest priority for M8 |
| 17 | A loosening policy change applies without anyone noticing | Loosening waits the current delay; the guardian of the policy in force can veto; tightening applies at once; changes emit events | Partial: tested on testnet (veto, early apply refused, apply after delay) |
| 18 | A lost or hostile guardian freezes the vault by vetoing every proposal and its own removal | The guardian cannot veto its own replacement; replacing or removing it waits max(2 x delay, 3 days), even with delay 0; the owner can cancel that change | Mitigated (unit + testnet). Trade-off: a thief with the owner key can remove the guardian after that wait |
| 19 | The signer's answer cannot be read (format change) or never arrives, and the vault stays locked forever | The callback reads the answer by hand and never panics; unreadable or failed answers are *uncertain* (nonce kept, no reuse for another transaction, proposal pinned); the owner can abandon a signature after 1 hour; a stale answer cannot land on another signature (the callback carries the hash) | Mitigated (unit tests; uncertain path triggered live with a 0 deposit) |
| 20 | A failed signature is retried and paid twice | A retry after an uncertain outcome is pinned to the same nonce and produces the identical transaction, so at most one can be mined; it is not charged twice | Mitigated (unit + live) |
| 21 | The policy moves to another EVM chain after signing and the nonce counter no longer matches that chain (unminable transactions) | The first signature fixes the vault's chain; `set_policy` and `apply_policy` refuse any other chain, also for a buyer; older vaults read it from their history | Mitigated (unit tests; vault 29 refuses a mainnet policy on testnet) |

## Out of scope

Legal status of selling an account, front-ends other than the reference page, chains other than one EVM test network, Bitcoin (one signature per UTXO).
